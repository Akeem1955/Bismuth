# Cellular Transport & Bioenergetics — Chapter Skill

## Summary
Membrane transport mechanisms and ATP energy conversion in chloroplasts and mitochondria.

## Chapter Objectives
- Contrast passive transport (diffusion, osmosis) with ATP-driven active transport
- Trace energy flow through photosynthesis and cellular respiration

## Topics in This Chapter
- **Cell Membrane: Osmosis, Diffusion & Active Transport** (`bio_cell_transport`): Selective permeability across the phospholipid bilayer and electrochemical gradient pumping.
- **Photosynthesis & Cellular Respiration Energy Cycles** (`bio_photosynthesis_atp`): Coupled energy conversion in chloroplasts (light reactions & Calvin cycle) and mitochondria.
