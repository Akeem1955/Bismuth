# Photosynthesis & Cellular Respiration Energy Cycles

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating the Bioenergetics lesson. Emphasize how the products of photosynthesis (glucose + O₂) serve as the exact reactants of aerobic respiration.

## Learning Objectives
1. Locate the light-dependent reactions (thylakoid) and Calvin cycle (stroma) in the chloroplast
2. Balance the overall photosynthesis and aerobic cellular respiration equations
3. Explain how NADPH and ATP link the light reactions to carbon fixation

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: Light-Dependent Reactions in the Thylakoid Membrane
- Step 2: Carbon Fixation in the Calvin Cycle (Stroma)
- Step 3: Complementary Aerobic Respiration in Mitochondria

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Believing plants only perform photosynthesis and do not have mitochondria for cellular respiration
- Thinking the oxygen O₂ released in photosynthesis comes from CO₂ rather than the splitting of water (H₂O)
