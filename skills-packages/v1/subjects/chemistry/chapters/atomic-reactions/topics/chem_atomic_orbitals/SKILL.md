# Atomic Structure, Isotopes & Electron Shells

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating the Atomic Structure lesson. Remind students that proton count Z uniquely locks the element identity.

## Learning Objectives
1. Define Atomic Number (Z = protons) and Mass Number (A = protons + neutrons)
2. Explain why isotopes of an element have identical chemical properties but different masses
3. Determine valence electron counts and ion charges from shell configurations

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: Nuclear Identity (Z and A)
- Step 2: Isotopes and Neutron Count (N = A - Z)
- Step 3: Electron Shells & Valence Reactivity

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Confusing mass number A with the number of neutrons N
- Adding electrons instead of subtracting electrons when forming a positively charged cation
