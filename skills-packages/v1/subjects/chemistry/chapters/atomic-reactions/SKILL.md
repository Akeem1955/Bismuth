# Atomic Structure & Quantitative Stoichiometry — Chapter Skill

## Summary
Subatomic composition, isotopes, molar mass conversions, and limiting reagents.

## Chapter Objectives
- Determine proton, neutron, and electron counts from atomic number Z and mass number A
- Balance chemical equations and identify limiting reagents using mole ratios

## Topics in This Chapter
- **Atomic Structure, Isotopes & Electron Shells** (`chem_atomic_orbitals`): Nuclear composition (Z, N, A), isotopic mass, and Bohr/orbital valence configurations.
- **Stoichiometry, Molar Mass & Limiting Reagents** (`chem_stoichiometry_moles`): Converting mass to moles (n = m/M) and using balanced coefficients to identify the limiting reactant.
