# Independent vs. Dependent Clauses & Complex Sentences

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating the Sentence Clauses lesson. Have the student locate the subject-verb pair and test whether the clause can stand alone as a complete sentence.

## Learning Objectives
1. Identify an Independent Clause (Subject + Finite Verb + Complete Thought)
2. Recognize how Subordinating Conjunctions (although, because, since, while) create Dependent Clauses
3. Correct comma splices using coordinating conjunctions, semicolons, or subordination

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: Independent Clause Anatomy (Complete Thought)
- Step 2: Dependent Clauses & Subordinating Conjunctions
- Step 3: Complex Punctuation & Fixing Comma Splices

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Joining two independent clauses with only a comma (creating a comma splice)
- Treating a long prepositional phrase as a clause when it lacks a finite verb
