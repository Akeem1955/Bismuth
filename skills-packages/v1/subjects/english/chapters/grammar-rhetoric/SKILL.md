# Clause Architecture & Rhetorical Voice — Chapter Skill

## Summary
Constructing compound-complex sentences and controlling agency through active and passive voice.

## Chapter Objectives
- Distinguish independent clauses from subordinate (dependent) clauses and punctuate them accurately
- Transform sentences between active and passive voice and choose the optimal voice for clarity

## Topics in This Chapter
- **Independent vs. Dependent Clauses & Complex Sentences** (`eng_sentence_clauses`): Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS), subordinating conjunctions, and eliminating comma splices.
- **Active vs. Passive Voice & Argumentative Precision** (`eng_active_passive_voice`): Grammatical agency, auxiliary 'be' + past participle transformations, and scientific objectivity.
