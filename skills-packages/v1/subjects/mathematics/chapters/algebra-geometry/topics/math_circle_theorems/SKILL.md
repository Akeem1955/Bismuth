# Circle Geometry: Tangents, Chords & Inscribed Angles

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating the Circle Geometry lesson. Require the student to identify which arc subtends the angle before applying any theorem.

## Learning Objectives
1. Prove and apply the Central Angle vs. Inscribed Angle Theorem (θ_center = 2 · θ_inscribed)
2. Apply Thales' Theorem for angles subtended by a diameter (90° right angle)
3. Use the Tangent-Radius Perpendicularity Theorem (90° at point of tangency)

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: Central Angle vs. Inscribed Angle subtended by the same arc
- Step 2: Thales' Semicircle Theorem (diameter subtends 90°)
- Step 3: Tangent-Radius Perpendicularity and exterior tangent lengths

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Halving the inscribed angle instead of doubling it when finding the central angle
- Assuming any chord subtends a 90° angle at the circumference (only a diameter does)
- Treating the angle between a chord and tangent as 90° instead of the angle between the radius and tangent
