# Foundations of Algebra & Geometry — Chapter Skill

## Summary
Equivalence transformations in linear equations and invariant angle relationships in circles.

## Chapter Objectives
- Solve multi-step single-variable linear equations using inverse operations
- Apply inscribed angle, chord, and tangent theorems to deduce unknown geometric measures

## Topics in This Chapter
- **Linear Equations & Balancing Operations** (`math_linear_equations`): Isolating variables using inverse operations while preserving equality across the balance scale.
- **Circle Geometry: Tangents, Chords & Inscribed Angles** (`math_circle_theorems`): Deductive angle relationships between central angles, inscribed angles, and tangent lines.
