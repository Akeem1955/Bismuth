# Mathematics — Subject Coordinator Skill

## Tagline
Symbolic reasoning, algebraic balance, and geometric proofs

## Teaching Philosophy
Guide students through mathematical structures step by step. Never give away the final numerical answer before the student identifies the invariant operation or geometric theorem.

## Chapters
- **Foundations of Algebra & Geometry** (`algebra-geometry`): Equivalence transformations in linear equations and invariant angle relationships in circles.
