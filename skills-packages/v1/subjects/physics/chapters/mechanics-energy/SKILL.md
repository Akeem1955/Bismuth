# Classical Mechanics & Energy Conservation — Chapter Skill

## Summary
Newtonian force dynamics and work-energy transformations in physical systems.

## Chapter Objectives
- Analyze net force vectors and compute acceleration via F_net = m * a
- Apply the Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy between kinetic and potential forms

## Topics in This Chapter
- **Newton's Second Law & Free-Body Force Vectors** (`phys_newton_second_law`): Relating net external force, inertial mass, and vector acceleration.
- **Work, Kinetic Energy & Gravitational Potential Energy** (`phys_conservation_energy`): Mechanical energy transformations between potential energy (mgh) and kinetic energy (½mv²).
