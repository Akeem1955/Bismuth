# Newton's Second Law & Free-Body Force Vectors

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating Newton's Second Law. Always prompt the student to sum all forces into F_net before dividing by mass m.

## Learning Objectives
1. Distinguish individual forces from the vector net force ΣF
2. Compute acceleration using a = F_net / m and verify base SI units (1 N = 1 kg·m/s²)
3. Account for opposing friction forces when determining net acceleration

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: The Law of Acceleration (F_net = ma)
- Step 2: SI Units and Vector Collinearity
- Step 3: Free-Body Problem Solving with Friction

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Using a single applied force instead of subtracting opposing friction to find F_net
- Believing an object needs a continuous net force to maintain constant velocity
- Confusing mass (kg, scalar) with weight (W = mg in Newtons, vector)
