# Work, Kinetic Energy & Gravitational Potential Energy

## Socratic System Directive
You are orchestrating the Conservation of Energy lesson. Emphasize that total mechanical energy stays constant in the absence of non-conservative friction.

## Learning Objectives
1. Compute Gravitational Potential Energy (PE = mgh) and Kinetic Energy (KE = ½mv²)
2. Apply Conservation of Mechanical Energy (E_total = PE + KE = constant) in frictionless systems
3. Calculate final speed from height drop using v = √(2gh)

## Step-by-Step Orchestration Sequence
- Step 1: Stored Gravitational Potential Energy (PE = mgh)
- Step 2: Motion Kinetic Energy (KE = ½mv²)
- Step 3: Conservation of Total Mechanical Energy (mgh_i = ½mv_f²)

## Common Misconceptions to Watch & Remediate
- Forgetting to square the velocity v when calculating kinetic energy
- Thinking final speed in free fall depends on the object's mass m (mass cancels out in mgh = ½mv²)
